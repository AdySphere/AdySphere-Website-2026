Most stalled Microsoft Dynamics 365 projects did not fail on technology. They failed because the system was configured around how someone assumed the business worked.

## What the X-Ray looks at

We go department by department and look at four things:

1. **Data.** Where it is created, where it is copied, and which version leadership actually trusts.
2. **Workflows.** What really happens, including the spreadsheet and the WhatsApp message.
3. **Handoffs.** Where work moves between teams, and where it waits.
4. **Reporting.** Which decisions arrive late, and why.

## What you get

A written report and a prioritised roadmap. Some fixes need Microsoft Dynamics 365. Some need a Power Automate flow. Some need a conversation, not software.

That is the point: you buy what fixes the problem, in the right order.
