An AI pilot that lives outside your systems creates a new job: someone has to copy data in, check the answer, and copy the result back.

## Three reasons pilots stall

- **No access to the real data.** The pilot sees an export, not the live record.
- **One bot per task.** Each one is blind to the rest of the business.
- **No owner for the output.** Nobody approves it, so nobody trusts it.

## What works instead

Put AI inside the systems your teams already use, under the same security roles, with a person approving the actions that matter. Start with one department where the payback is clear, prove it, then extend.
